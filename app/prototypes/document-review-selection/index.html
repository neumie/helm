Dashboard for the lead therapist

The lead therapist needs a first dashboard of the therapists on their team. Each row shows the therapist’s name and, for the chosen period, how many processes are planned, occupied, archived, blocked, and still open.

What K1 means

K1 is the date of the first ketamine session. For every other product, use the date of the first session in that process. Do not ask which project this belongs to.

Remaining to fill is planned minus occupied, archived, and blocked. A blocked process is one with an unconfirmed absence or holiday conflict. Archive reasons stay out of the summary table; the row opens a detail where those reasons are counted.

The period filter uses K1 inside the selected year. Switching therapist or period should not reload the whole workspace.
